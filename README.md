# Tsadi

Product Compliance & Safety Executive with about two decades of certification and safety engineering experience across EV battery systems, BESS, and robotics hardware. Now building the tooling that makes that work faster and harder to get wrong.

## Background

Certification and safety work across FCC, UL, IEC, and CE/RED schemes. Hands-on experience at Ample, Pax Labs, Safety Engineering Laboratory, Silicon Valley Compliance, and Kodak/Creo. I run "Product Safety and Compliance," a 6,000+ member LinkedIn community for people doing this work.

## Projects

- [bess-safety-margin](https://github.com/tsadi/bess-safety-margin): thermal margin and cell-to-cell propagation risk screening for BESS pack designs, built on first-principles thermal engineering, not fabricated standard thresholds.
- [robot-risk-register](https://github.com/tsadi/robot-risk-register): ISO 12100-style hazard risk register generator for robotics and AMR safety programs, with a CI-wireable acceptance gate.
- [compliance-gap-finder](https://github.com/tsadi/compliance-gap-finder): checklist-based gap screening for technical documents and test reports, with an optional Claude-assisted second pass.

Every project above ships with tests, a documented methodology, and an explicit "what this is not" section. No fabricated numbers, no hardcoded pass/fail thresholds pretending to be standards text.
